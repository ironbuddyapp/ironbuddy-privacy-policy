IronBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026
App: IronBuddy (Android package com.ironbuddy.app)
Contact: iron.buddy.app@gmail.com

IronBuddy (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a local workout planning and logging app. This Privacy Policy describes what information the App handles, how it is stored, and what we do not collect. It is intended to meet Google Play requirements for health and fitness apps, including clear disclosure of on-device data and user-initiated exports.

1. Summary

  • Workout and fitness data you enter stays on your device.
  • No IronBuddy account is required and we do not operate a cloud sync backend for your workouts.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • The App does not include ads or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Files leave your device only if you choose Save, Share, or Auto-backup to local storage.

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to users of the IronBuddy Android application distributed on Google Play (and related testing tracks). It covers data processed by the App on your device. It does not cover third-party apps or services you later send files to (for example email, Google Drive, or messaging apps).

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores information you provide or generate while using it in on-device storage (application storage / local storage). Depending on how you use IronBuddy, this may include:

  • Fitness / workout information: training splits and schedules, exercises, sets, reps, weights, workout history, notes, and progress-related calculations (for example estimated strength metrics derived from your logged lifts).
  • Health and fitness data: body weight and body fat percentage entries (optional), and workout/training logs.
  • Personal info you enter: an optional display name and unit preferences (kg/lb).
  • App settings: preferences such as whether automatic JSON backup is enabled.
  • Purchase information: IronBuddy is sold as a one-time paid app on Google Play. Your purchase is processed entirely by Google Play at the point of download — the App itself does not process, receive, or store any payment or transaction information.

This information is used only to provide App features (planning workouts, logging sessions, showing progress charts, and optional local backup). We do not receive this data on IronBuddy servers because the App does not upload your training data to an IronBuddy cloud service.

4. Purchases and payment processing

IronBuddy is a paid app — you pay once through Google Play to download it, and there is no separate in-app purchase or subscription. This transaction is handled entirely by Google Play as part of the download process, before the App is installed on your device. The App contains no payment processing code and does not receive, access, or store any payment, billing, or transaction information.

For details on how Google Play handles your payment data, see Google Play's own Privacy Policy.

5. Data we do not collect

As of the last updated date above, IronBuddy does not:

  • Create user accounts or require sign-in
  • Collect data onto IronBuddy-operated servers
  • Use advertising SDKs or sell personal data
  • Use third-party analytics / crash SDKs that report your workouts to us
  • Access your contacts, precise location, microphone, or camera

6. Exports, backups, and sharing (user-initiated)

You may optionally save or share data using Android system features:

  • JSON backup: a file (typically under Downloads / IronBuddy) containing App data so you can restore later. Auto-backup, if enabled, updates that local file after changes.
  • Share: system share sheet for JSON or a PDF of your active split.
  • Import / restore: you may choose a backup file to load into the App.

These actions are started by you. Once a file is saved or shared, the destination (Downloads folder, email, Drive, messaging apps, etc.) is outside IronBuddy’s control and subject to that destination’s policies. Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete files already saved in Downloads.

7. Permissions

The App may request or declare permissions such as:

  • Internet / network: used by the Android WebView/OS networking stack (for example, if you open external links such as open-source credits). The App does not use this to upload your workout, body, or personal data.
  • Storage / media access (as required by Android version): to save or read backup files when you use backup, restore, or share features.

Exact permission labels shown by Android may vary by OS version. Unused sensitive permissions are not requested for core logging features.

8. How you can delete data

  • In-app: Settings → Reset All Data clears App data stored by IronBuddy on the device and returns you to setup.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling removes the App’s private storage.
  • Backup files: JSON files you saved under Downloads (or shared elsewhere) must be deleted manually if you no longer want those copies.

Because we do not host your workout data on IronBuddy servers, there is no separate cloud account deletion request for training logs. For privacy questions, email iron.buddy.app@gmail.com.

9. Health and fitness notice

IronBuddy is a fitness logging and planning tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Progress charts and estimated strength figures are informational calculations based on data you enter, not clinical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use IronBuddy if you are under the age required in your country to consent to use of local apps of this type.

11. Third-party services

If you open external links from the App (for example open-source credits) or share files through other apps, those services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies. IronBuddy does not control them.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or our practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

13. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: iron.buddy.app@gmail.com
